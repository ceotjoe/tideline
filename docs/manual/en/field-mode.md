# Field mode

For operating outdoors: on a summit, in a park, in the sun. **Settings → Field mode** has one switch that sets four
things together:

- the **sunlight theme** (maximum contrast),
- **glove mode** (larger buttons and spacing),
- the **battery saver**,
- **keep the screen on while logging**.

Switching it off puts your earlier theme and button size back. Each of the four can also be set on its own, so you can
have glove mode with your usual theme. If you change one of them by hand, the main switch shows *off*.

## What the battery saver does
- The tide at the top stands still. The number of waiting QSOs is still shown as text.
- The clock in the entry form updates once a minute (it shows minutes) instead of every second.
- The contest rates are recomputed every 30 seconds instead of every 5.

Tideline never worked in the background, and the saver does not change when it syncs: when you open the app, when the
network comes back and when you tap *Sync now*. It saves less than switching the screen off does; the biggest saving is
a shorter screen time, which is why the next option costs battery.

## Keeping the screen on
While the log, Fast Log Entry or the contest screen is open, the screen does not switch off. It switches off as usual
everywhere else and when you leave the app. This uses more battery; turn it off if you need every hour. Nothing is
sent anywhere for this.

## Not included
There is no button that fills in your locator from the phone's location. Typing it, or using Fast Log Entry, still works.
