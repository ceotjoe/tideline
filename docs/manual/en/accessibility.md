# Accessibility features

- **Screen readers.** VoiceOver, TalkBack and Narrator are supported.
  - Callsigns are read **letter by letter**.
  - Every logged QSO is announced.
  - The sync level is spoken as text.
- **Large text.** Tideline follows your system text size up to 200 %.
- **Easy-to-read font.** **Settings → Easy-to-read font** switches to Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed so that
  characters such as 0 and O, or 1, l and I, can't be confused.
- **Extra text spacing.** Wider letter, word and line spacing.
- **Themes.**
  - **Sunlight** gives maximum contrast outdoors.
  - **Night red** keeps your night vision; also turn your screen brightness down.
  - All themes meet the WCAG 2.2 AA contrast rules. Sunlight even meets the stricter AAA level.
- **Glove mode.** Larger buttons and spacing.
- **Colour is never the only signal.** Every status has an icon and a word.
- **Reduce motion.** When this is on in your system settings, the tide wave stays still.
- **Keyboard.** Everything can be reached with a keyboard. Press Ctrl/⌘ + / or F1 for all shortcuts.
