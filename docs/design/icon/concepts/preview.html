Tideline app icon — concepts

Tide motif plus radio element, built as separate flat layers for Apple's Liquid Glass (background + 2 foreground layers). Columns: default, dark, clear and tinted appearance.

A · Signal sun

A transmitter rising like the sun over the tide: a dot with radio arcs above two calm wave lines.

A vertical antenna standing in the tide line, radiating to both sides — portable operation at the shore.

C · Swell to signal

One continuous line: a slow ocean swell on the left becomes a tight radio carrier on the right, above the horizon.
